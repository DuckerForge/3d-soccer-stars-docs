Privacy Policy — 3D Soccer Stars

Effective date: June 12, 2026 · App: 3D Soccer Stars (xyz.sbfootball.app)

1. Summary

3D Soccer Stars collects the minimum data needed to run its optional online features. No advertising, no tracking SDKs, no sale of personal data. Playing offline (guest mode) sends us nothing.

2. Data we process

  • Solana wallet public address — only if you choose to connect a wallet. Used as your account identifier for cloud saves and the leaderboard. A wallet address is pseudonymous; we never receive your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Game progress — career stage, card collection, coins, match results, and the display name you choose for the leaderboard. Stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database), keyed by your wallet address.
  • Purchase log — when you buy coins with SKR we record the on-chain transaction signature, bundle and timestamp for support and auditing. The transaction itself is public on the Solana blockchain by design.
  • Local data — settings and progress are also stored on your device only (app private storage).

3. What we do NOT collect

No name, email, phone, contacts, location, advertising ID, biometrics or device fingerprinting. No third-party analytics or ads SDKs.

4. Where data lives & retention

Online data is stored in Google Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure; see Firebase Privacy). We keep it while your account is active. You can request deletion at any time (see below) and we will remove your profile, progress and leaderboard entries. On-chain transactions cannot be deleted by anyone — that is a property of public blockchains.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Your chosen display name, team rating and results appear on the public leaderboard. Service providers: Google Firebase (storage), Solana RPC (blockchain access via your wallet).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Wallet features require a wallet app, which itself imposes age requirements.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data (GDPR/CCPA where applicable) by emailing cismytb@gmail.com from a message containing your wallet address, or by disconnecting your wallet in-game and requesting erasure.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy are published at this address with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Data controller — the developer of 3D Soccer Stars: cismytb@gmail.com
